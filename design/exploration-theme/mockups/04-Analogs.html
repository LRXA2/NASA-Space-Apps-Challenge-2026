Limits of this page

  • Cosine compares shapes the model learned. It is not a percentage, an accuracy or a suitability rating.
  • Matches come only from five prepared Earth sites. A closer analog could exist elsewhere on Earth.
  • Earth cannot reproduce lunar gravity, vacuum, radiation, lighting or regolith. This rehearses route planning only.
  • Check tolerances are the similarity pipeline's own comparison bands, not mission requirements.
