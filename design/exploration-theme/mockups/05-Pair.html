Limits of this page

  • Matching terrain shape doesn't make Atacama lunar: gravity is six times stronger, there is air and weather, and the surface is soil and rock.
  • Features smaller than about 30 m, such as boulders and small craters, are invisible in both grids.
  • The tolerances are the similarity pipeline's comparison bands, not mission requirements.
  • Whether a crew could actually rehearse at Atacama (climate, land cover, access) has not been scored.
