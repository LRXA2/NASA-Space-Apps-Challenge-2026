# Stage 3 — Environmental Suitability Proposal

**Current existing-data prototype scope (9 November 2026):** mission/equipment profiles are optional, not prerequisites for the default environmental comparison. Step 4's [scoped rule register](research/environment_rule_register.json) and the Step 5 [evidence/explanation handoff](research/STEP_4_5_PROTOTYPE.md) supplement the decisions below. Global/orbital, historical and ROI-center evidence is context only; no new score factors, equipment defaults or operational passes are inferred. Existing exploratory thresholds/weights remain explicitly distinct from sourced operational requirements.

**Primary objective:** evaluate whether an intended mission can operate under the environmental conditions at a selected site, as stated in [outline Stage 3](../outline.md#stage-3--environmental-and-equipment-suitability-engine). This is the **Required Stage 3 target**, not an optional extension. Our active implementation is Moon-focused; Mars cannot use lunar evidence.

**Order of work:** required Stage 3 requirements and evidence gaps → original research P1 → P2 → P3. Required Stage 3 is a workstream overlay, **not a rewrite of the research CSV's P tags**. Additional data is useful only if its location, time, quantity and decision relevance are established.

Missing inputs are analyzed as readiness gaps, not presented as completed numerical mission assessments.

## 4. Implemented versus demonstrated

[Pipeline_Proto](Pipeline_Proto/README.md) preserves the terrain CNN's notebook/configuration/verified-artifact structure and uses the sample's eligibility → scoring → recommendations separation. **Original terrain CNN code and tensors are unchanged.** required Stage 3 audit/time/reference outputs are consumed and pinned under `stage3_evidence/`, not merely mentioned in this document.

Conditional numerical checks now cover consumable stock, chronological storage reserve, component thermal envelopes, communications outage and explicit quantity/unit-matched capacity–demand margins. Their operation is tested with synthetic fixtures, **not misrepresented as real mission evidence**.

Diagnostic proxy rankings remain separate from `stage3_status` and `stage3_top_k.csv`. A complete, applicable mandatory requirement set must pass before configured operational preferences are ranked. No supplied mission/equipment profile currently satisfies that prerequisite.

## 5. What else is needed

1. **Project inputs first:** chosen site/support, dates/duration/activities, crew, equipment, capabilities, reserves, complete applicable requirements and their sources. Use the blank [mission profile contract](Pipeline_Proto/mission_profile_template.json); it is not a proposed mission.
2. **Applicable environmental/response evidence:** dated terrain-aware generation/contact; actual component thermal response; local ground properties/loads; applicable radiation transport and protection evidence; other activity-specific constraints. Existing broad archives do not automatically provide these.
3. **Targeted retrieval:** a useful dated polar Diviner subset needs quality, footprint and requested-period selection; current D20 pilots are inadequate. D21–D24 remain KIV. S01 full PDF acquisition remains blocked in the local transport despite the available verified-source extract.
4. **Validation:** operational model adequacy, conservative temporal/spatial support and justified preferences must be evaluated before final mission/location claims. No reference or sensitivity result is adopted as a universal safe-site rule.
