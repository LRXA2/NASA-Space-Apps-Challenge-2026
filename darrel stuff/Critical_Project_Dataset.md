# Environmental Dataset Register

**Current focus:** environmental evidence for Earth and Moon locations. This register also distinguishes supporting land-cover/quality sources and optional environmental/resource sources mentioned elsewhere in the repository. Terrain-model development and access/logistics remain outside scope. Mars references are historical and listed separately as excluded. Environmental variables are not inputs to the current terrain CNN.

## Status and evidence

The acquisition counts below come from the [download checklist](../DOWNLOAD_CHECKLIST.md), whose acquisition snapshot is **30 October 2026** and project-status update is **2 November 2026**, together with the saved notebook results. The initial review on **5 November 2026** found no `data/` directory; `data/raw/` now exists, but its contents, completeness and integrity have **not** been audited. Existing data is left untouched. Recorded completion does not establish current local availability. The original `/data/` is excluded by [the repository ignore file](../.gitignore). The environmental downloader stores new data in **`darrel stuff/data/`**, excluded by [`darrel stuff/.gitignore`](.gitignore).

### Priority and progress meanings

These priorities reflect the current environmental focus, not acquisition completeness:

- **P1 — Core:** primary Earth climate, lunar temperature and lunar illumination/visibility evidence. These are the four originally selected entries, spanning three dataset families.
- **P2 — Conditional support/alternative:** existing environmental-quality or land-cover context, or a climate/screening alternative to consider only for a specific unresolved question. Not an automatic acquisition requirement.
- **P3 — Optional resource study:** lunar resource/mineral evidence requiring a defined scientific question and product selection before acquisition.
- **Excluded:** historical Mars sources; no acquisition in the active Earth–Moon scope.

**Recorded acquired/processed** refers to repository records, not files verified here. **No acquisition recorded** does not prove that nobody has a private copy. **Selected layers acquired** does not establish acquisition of every product in an archive. Restore existing data where available before considering reacquisition.

The [environmental downloader](download_script/download_environmental.py)'s requested core scope remains **P1 only**. Adding a source to this register does not authorize downloading every P2/P3 or historical source. See the [P1 download instructions](download_script/README.md) for offline previews, inventories and downloads. Downloader code lives in `darrel stuff/download_script/`; default datasets, partials and manifests go under **`darrel stuff/data/`**. The script creates that data directory if missing and reuses it otherwise; `--dry-run` creates nothing. Original project data paths below are not modified.

## Interpretation limits and remaining environmental work

- **Earth temperature is air temperature; Diviner measures lunar surface brightness temperature.** They are not interchangeable quantities. TerraClimate `tmin`/`tmax` are monthly averages of minimum/maximum temperatures, not daily extremes; `ppt` is a monthly total.
- **Diviner phase-bin averages are not mission-time extremes or a continuous time series.** Preserve missing cells and bins. Requiring complete thermal coverage can exclude entire regions rather than repair missing evidence.
- **Average illumination does not establish continuous-darkness duration.** Earth visibility, solar visibility, permanent shadow and sky visibility remain separate products. Retain documented units and unresolved quality/unit holds.
- **Preserve native spatial and temporal support.** Environmental fields must not be treated as independent 30 m terrain measurements or collapsed into an unsupported suitability score.
- **Next environmental decision:** define the activity's duration, season and required conditions before selecting additional evidence such as daily extremes or mission-window darkness. These are scenario-dependent gaps, not a requirement for another broad download sweep. Environmental/equipment suitability integration remains unfinished.
